The [[glossary/decision-record]] status meaning the decision passed
review and is in effect. A record becomes accepted only through a decision
review that meets the [[glossary/review-quorum]] within the
[[glossary/review-window]]. Accepted records are also the machine-readable
work queue the Adopt-stage engine consumes.
